Free Pomodoro timer with focus and break sessions and an alarm.

About the Pomodoro Timer

Work in focused sessions followed by short breaks. The timer plays an alarm when a session ends, which suits studying and work.

How to use the Pomodoro Timer

  1. Choose focus or break.
  2. Start the timer and work without distractions.
  3. Take a break when the alarm rings.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Pomodoro technique?

You focus for 25 minutes, take a 5-minute break, and after four rounds take a longer break.

Does the alarm work on a phone?

Yes, but keep the tab open and your phone's sound on so that you hear it.
