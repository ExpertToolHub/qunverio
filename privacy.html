Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This policy explains what information Qunverio (“we”, “us”) handles when you use https://qunverio.vercel.app. We try to collect as little as possible.

Your files and inputs

Our calculators and our photo, image and PDF tools work inside your web browser. The numbers you type and the files you select are processed on your device and are not uploaded to our servers. If a tool ever needs an outside service, it will say so on that tool’s page.

Information stored on your device

We use your browser’s local storage to remember things like your favourite tools, recently used tools, theme (light or dark) and site settings. You can clear this at any time from your browser settings.

Information we may collect

  • Anonymous usage counts such as how many times a tool page is opened. These counts are not linked to your name or identity.
  • Analytics. We may use Google Analytics to understand which pages are popular. It may use cookies and collects information such as your approximate location, device and browser. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • Feedback. If you send us a message, we store the message and any contact detail you choose to include so that we can reply.

Third-party services

Qunverio is hosted on Vercel. We load fonts from Google Fonts and some code libraries from cdnjs. We use Google Firebase to store site settings, anonymous counters and feedback. These services may receive technical information such as your IP address when your browser connects to them. Please read their own privacy policies for details.

Advertising

We do not currently show advertisements. If we add advertising in the future, we will update this policy and explain how cookies are used.

Children

Qunverio is a general-purpose tool site and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can block cookies, clear local storage, or use a private window. You can ask us to delete a feedback message you sent by writing to techrakesh196@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Write to techrakesh196@gmail.com.
