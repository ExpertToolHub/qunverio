Questions

What is Qunverio?

Qunverio is a free online toolbox with calculators, PDF tools, image tools, generators and AI helpers. It works on mobile and desktop with no sign-up.

How do I compress a photo to 20 KB, 50 KB or 100 KB?

Open the Photo Compressor to 20/50/100 KB tool, choose your photo, tap the KB size you need and download the result.

Can I make a rent receipt for HRA here?

Yes. Use the Rent Receipt Generator, fill in the tenant, landlord, rent and month, and download the receipt as PDF or PNG.

Can I type Hindi using English letters?

Yes. The Hindi Typing tool turns Hinglish such as "namaste" into Hindi text that you can copy.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Image, PDF and text tools run in your browser, so your files stay on your device.

Do I need to install an app?

No. Qunverio runs in your browser. Open it on any phone or computer and start using the tools.
